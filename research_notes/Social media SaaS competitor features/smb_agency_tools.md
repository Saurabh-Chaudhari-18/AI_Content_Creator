Research date: 2026-11-04. Unless otherwise stated, "vendor pricing page, fetched 2026-11-04" means the live page was read on that date. Several vendor pages render prices client-side and returned blank price fields (Sendible, Zoho Social, Publer redirect loop); for those, third-party trackers with their own verification dates are cited and flagged. ## Key Question 1: Entry price, price per additional profile/user/brand, and agency/white-label tier cost and inclusions (per tool)

### Takeaway
Pricing models split into four camps: per-channel (Buffer, Publer), per-workspace/brand (Planable, Sendible, Metricool, Zoho Social, SocialBee), per-user (Agorapulse), and bundled profile+seat tiers (SocialPilot, Vista Social, Loomly, Later). Agency-grade white-labelling is a paid add-on at Sendible (roughly $268-$315/mo), Custom-only at Metricool, Ultimate/Enterprise at SocialPilot, Scale ($449/mo) at Vista Social, Beyond ($249-$332/mo) at Loomly, and Agency ($230/mo annual per 2022/2026 third-party data) at Zoho Social; Buffer, Later, Planable, Publer and SocialBee do not offer true white-label.

## Key Question 2: Client approval workflows — which tools, and how they work

### Takeaway
Planable is the only tool whose core product is built around approvals (None/Optional/Required/Multi-level, per-post comments, annotations, version history, no-login guest approvers). Vista Social, SocialPilot, Sendible, Loomly and Agorapulse offer client approval at mid/upper tiers; SocialPilot and Vista Social emphasise no-login link-based approval; Agorapulse and Metricool gate approvals to higher tiers; Buffer, Later and SocialBee offer only internal/basic approvals; Publer's approval capability is not documented in sources consulted.

### Cited Findings
- **Planable**: Basic plan approval types None/Optional; Pro adds Required; Enterprise adds Multi-level ("stacked approval steps, e.g., team lead, client, legal"). Version history 1 week (Basic), 30 days (Pro), unlimited (Enterprise). Pro adds team-only drafts/notes and bulk request for approval — [Planable pricing](https://planable.io/pricing/)
- Planable approvers are "invited by email or link"; "Occasional approvers don't need an account to view posts and comment"; permissions set individually to view/approve/edit/publish/analyze; posts can stay hidden from clients until ready; feedback via annotations and text suggestions; comments resolvable; changes tracked and revertable; approved posts auto-schedule; bulk approve in list view across workspaces; notifications via email, push, Slack — [Planable approval feature page](https://planable.io/features/approval/)
- **Loomly**: Starter includes "Approval workflows and roles"; Beyond adds "Custom roles & workflows" — [Loomly pricing](https://www.loomly.com/pricing); features page lists custom approval workflows, post mockups, roles and permissions, real-time commenting, version history, Slack/Teams notifications — [Loomly features](https://www.loomly.com/features)
- **Sendible**: "Assignment & approvals" first appears on Plus; however the feature table lists "Client dashboards," "Assign posts for approval," and "Custom approval workflows" for every tier including Core — page is internally inconsistent — [Sendible pricing](https://www.sendible.com/pricing). Approval emails can be sent from the agency's custom domain on white label — [Sendible white label](https://www.sendible.com/white-label)
- **SocialPilot**: Client approval described as "Enable hassle-free content approval without the need for login credentials"; clients get direct links by email, review from any device, all pending content on one screen; personalized invitation links onboard clients — [SocialPilot client management](https://www.socialpilot.co/features/client-management). Client Approval Workflows on Premium and Ultimate only; Standard has "Content Approval Workflows" and "Manager Approval Workflow" (internal) — [SocialPilot pricing](https://www.socialpilot.co/pricing)
- **Vista Social**: workflows can have one or more steps; non-logged-in clients review via a shared calendar link (external approval); internal and external steps can be combined (team handles first 1-2 steps, client final approval via link); external reviewers leave external comments but typically do not edit — [Vista Social approval workflow guide](https://vistasocial.com/insights/social-media-post-approval-workflow-in-vista-social/); [Vista Social support: Collaboration & Approvals](https://support.vistasocial.com/hc/en-us/sections/9761420374299-Collaboration-Approvals). Single-stage on Professional, multi-stage on Advanced — [socialbyidm](https://socialbyidm.com/social-media-management-tools-for-agencies/)
- **Agorapulse**: Standard has no approvals; Professional/Advanced "Simple approval workflows" (one-step); multi-step only on Custom — [Agorapulse pricing](https://www.agorapulse.com/pricing/)
- **Metricool**: "Post approval system" only on Advanced and Custom — [Metricool pricing](https://metricool.com/pricing/); roundup notes "Approvals arrive late" (i.e., high in the tier ladder) and approvals are "limited for client work" — [socialbyidm](https://socialbyidm.com/social-media-management-tools-for-agencies/); [Planable scheduling-tools roundup](https://planable.io/blog/schedule-social-media-posts/)
- **Buffer**: approval workflows only on Team ($10/channel); team members draft and submit, "Admins or managers can review, edit, and approve"; access levels from full posting to approval-only — [Buffer for Agencies](https://buffer.com/partners/agencies)
- **Later**: Growth and Scale include "internal and external approvals" and an "external comment workflow"; Starter excludes them — [Later pricing](https://later.com/pricing/)
- **SocialBee**: approvals on Accelerate and Pro (not Bootstrap); internal notes Pro only; review pending posts by profile or content source, team notes and @-mentions — [SocialBee pricing](https://socialbee.com/pricing/); [SocialBee features](https://socialbee.com/features/)
- **Zoho Social**: "Workflow & approvals" and custom roles from Premium upward; Agency tiers add client invites and free client logins — [Zoho Social plan comparison](https://www.zoho.com/social/plan-comparison.html)
- **Publer**: approval workflow not mentioned in pricing-focused sources — [Blotato Publer pricing](https://www.blotato.com/blog/publer-pricing)

### Inferences
- Tools that let clients approve without a login (Planable guest approvers, SocialPilot email links, Vista Social shared calendar links) reduce onboarding friction; Zoho and Sendible rely on client logins/dashboards instead.
- Multi-level approval is a premium gate everywhere: Planable Enterprise, Agorapulse Custom, Vista Social Advanced, Loomly Beyond (custom workflows).
