# Market Trends and Platform Rules for a B2B Social Media Management SaaS (2026)

Research date: 2026-11-04. Items that changed during 2025-2026 are dated inline. Platform API figures are quoted from the official developer docs where the page could be fetched; where only secondary sources were reachable this is flagged.

## Key Question 1: Market size, growth, and who is winning/losing (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
Analyst estimates for the social media management software market in 2025 range from roughly USD 5B (narrow software-only scope) to USD 31B (broad scope), with 14-26% CAGR; no single consensus figure exists. Structurally, 2025-2026 was a consolidation and distress period: Later bought Mavely (Jan 2025, $250M), Sprout bought NewsWhip (Jul 2025, up to $65M), Hootsuite cut ~20% of staff (Oct 2025) and swapped CEOs (Apr 2026), while Sprout Social's stock fell sharply despite ~$457M FY2025 revenue.

### Inferences
- The incumbents are investing via M&A in two directions: (1) creator/affiliate commerce (Later-Mavely, Sprout-Tagger) and (2) listening/media intelligence + AI agents (Hootsuite-Talkwalker, Sprout-NewsWhip, Sprout Trellis). Pure scheduling is commoditising (Buffer at $0-10/channel, Metricool, Planable), which is why Buffer is restructuring plans downward.
- Hootsuite's layoffs, CEO churn and reputational controversy in 2025-2026 create a window for challengers in the SMB/mid-market tier, but Hootsuite remains perceived as the category leader in buyer surveys.
- A new entrant should pick a scope-aligned market number for planning: ~USD 5-6B (software licences only, QYResearch) is the defensible TAM; the USD 20-30B figures include services and adjacent analytics.

## Key Question 2: 2025-2026 feature trends and where incumbents are investing

### Takeaway
Both flagship reports converge on: AI is table stakes but consumers punish visible AI in brand content; authenticity, employee voices and ongoing creator partnerships outperform follower-count metrics; social is now a search/discovery and customer-care channel; and social listening/"social intelligence" is being repositioned as first-party data and business intelligence.

### Cited Findings
Hootsuite Social Trends 2026 (survey of 6,500+ marketers):
- Survey base: over 6,500 marketers and social media professionals plus platform data — [ThinkDigital summary](https://www.thinkdigital.travel/research-directory/hootsuite-social-trends-2026)
- 17 named trends across four categories: Culture and attention (chaos culture, work-life balance, nostalgic remix, cozy aesthetic, micro-drama); Creative acceleration and AI (micro-behavior, analytics, rapid-response "fastvertising", authenticity, AI-native); Influence and performance (performance partnerships, humanizing brands, employee advocacy); Brand intelligence and strategy (social intelligence, side quest, creativity on LinkedIn and Substack, search-first) — [Hootsuite Social Trends](https://www.hootsuite.com/research/social-trends)
- Nearly a third of consumers say they are less likely to choose a brand that uses AI ads; AI-generated articles surpassed human-written articles online for the first time in 2025 — [Hootsuite Social Trends](https://www.hootsuite.com/research/social-trends)
- Audiences trust employees more than influencers or CEOs (Edelman, cited by Hootsuite); brands are shifting from one-off creator posts to ongoing partnerships measured on ROI; follower count and engagement rate are "no longer considered reliable indicators of impact" — [Hootsuite Social Trends](https://www.hootsuite.com/research/social-trends)
- Algorithms no longer reward follower accumulation; hover time and rewatching are key signals — [Hootsuite report PDF mirror](https://adindex.ru/publication/analitics/search/344386/img/HootsuiteSocialTrends2026_Report_en.pdf)
- Social platforms are becoming sources of consent-based first-party data via lead-gen ads, subscriptions, gated content, live events and DMs — [Hootsuite Social Trends](https://www.hootsuite.com/research/social-trends)
- Context stats in the report: Gen X spent $15.2T in 2025; TikTok 45+ users grew ~1,200% 2019-2025; Deloitte projects micro-dramas at $7.8B revenue — [Hootsuite Social Trends](https://www.hootsuite.com/research/social-trends)

Sprout Social Index 2025 (Edition XX):
- Methodology: Glimpse survey, Sept 13-27, 2024; 4,044 consumers (follow 5+ brands), 900 social practitioners, 322 marketing leaders; US, UK, Canada, Australia — [Sprout Social Index](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/index)
- Three headline themes: social as "the epicenter of culture" (understand cultural moments, don't recreate every meme); AI proficiency to fight creative burnout and scale output; an executive trust gap where leaders claim to understand social's impact but social teams are skeptical — [Sprout Social Index](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/index)
- A third of consumers think jumping on viral trends is embarrassing for brands — [Sprout press release via Mammoth Times](https://business.mammothtimes.com/mammothtimes/article/gnwcq-2025-1-7-the-days-of-trend-chasing-are-over-new-research-from-sprout-social-reveals-a-third-of-consumers-think-jumping-on-viral-trends-is-embarrassing-for-brands)
- Comments, @-mentions and DMs are consumers' preferred customer-service channels; 71% of social users would buy from a competitor if a brand does not answer service questions (secondary summary) — [Marketing Beat](https://www.marketing-beat.co.uk/2025/01/08/sprout-social-data); [Sprout customer service stats](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-customer-service-statistics/)
- Sprout CMO: "Largely driven by Gen Z, social is becoming the new search engine" — [GuruFocus](https://gurufocus.com/news/2648370/sprout-social-inc-spt-unveils-2025-social-media-trends-and-strategies)
- Sprout Q4 2025 Pulse Survey (Nov 2025): 69% of consumers (78% of Gen Z/Millennials) are comfortable with AI used for faster social customer care; 73% of shoppers planned to contact a brand on social during the holiday season — [Sprout press release via Mammoth Times](https://business.mammothtimes.com/mammothtimes/article/gnwcq-2025-11-4-social-media-overtakes-traditional-channels-for-holiday-shopping-inspiration)

Employee advocacy market:
- Employee advocacy software market estimates for 2025 range from ~$587M to USD 1.8B (22.6% CAGR to 2032 per Coherent) depending on scope — [NextMSC](https://www.nextmsc.com/report/employee-advocacy-software-market-ic5950); [Coherent via Owler/Openpr](https://www.owler.com/reports/hootsuite/hootsuite--employee-advocacy-tool-market-size-2025/1744615693095); [Credence Research](https://www.credenceresearch.com/report/employee-advocacy-software-market)

### Inferences
- Product priorities implied for 2026: (1) AI assist that is invisible to the end audience (drafting, repurposing, analytics) rather than AI-visible creative; (2) first-class employee advocacy and creator-partnership workflows; (3) unified inbox / social customer care with SLA tracking; (4) listening repositioned as competitive and cultural intelligence; (5) search-first optimisation (keywords/captions for in-platform search) over hashtag strategies; (6) measurement decoupled from follower counts (watch time, saves, conversions).
- Incumbent AI investments (Sprout Trellis, Hootsuite OwlyGPT/Talkwalker) target analytics agents, leaving room for an SMB-priced product that wraps publishing + care + advocacy.

## Key Question 3: Hard technical and policy constraints of each platform's publishing API

### Takeaway
Every major network now gates third-party publishing behind app review and quotas: Instagram 100 posts/24h per account, Threads 250 posts/24h, YouTube effectively 100 uploads/day per project, X charges per post ($0.015, $0.20 with URL), TikTok forces private-only posts until a separate audit, LinkedIn starts apps at 500 calls/day until Standard-tier upgrade, Google Business Profile defaults to 300 QPM and 10 edits/min per profile, and Pinterest trial apps get 1,000 calls/day with sandbox-only pins.

### Cited Findings

Instagram (Meta Graph API / Instagram Platform):
- "Instagram accounts are limited to 100 API-published posts within a 24-hour moving period"; carousels count as one post; enforced on `POST /<IG_ID>/media_publish`; check usage with `GET /<IG_ID>/content_publishing_limit` — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing)
- Note: the carousel section of the same page states 50 posts per 24 hours, which conflicts with the 100 figure on the page — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing)
- Supported via API: single JPEG images, feed videos, Reels (`media_type=REELS`, optional `trial_params` for trial Reels), carousels of up to 20 images/videos (`CAROUSEL` container; all images cropped to first image's aspect ratio, default 1:1), Stories (`media_type=STORIES`) — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing)
- Not supported: extended JPEG formats (MPO, JPS), shopping tags, filters; alt text not supported for Reels or Stories; media must be on a publicly accessible server at publish time; resumable uploads only for Facebook Login for Business apps — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing)
- `alt_text` field for image posts added March 24, 2025; `is_ai_generated=true` self-discloses AI usage (set on the carousel container for carousels) — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing)
- Account must be an Instagram professional account; Pages requiring Page Publishing Authorization cannot publish until PPA is complete — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing)
- Permissions: Instagram Login: `instagram_business_basic`, `instagram_business_content_publish`; Facebook Login: `instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`, `pages_read_engagement` (+ `ads_management`, `ads_read` if Business Manager role); access levels Advanced or Standard — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing)
- Scheduling is not an API feature; Meta recommends apps enforce the rate limit themselves if they offer scheduling; poll container `status_code` once per minute for no more than 5 minutes — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing)
- Instagram Login scopes renamed; old `business_*` scope values deprecated as of January 27, 2025 — [Meta: Business Login for Instagram](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/business-login)

Facebook Pages API:
- Scheduled posts: set `published=false` and `scheduled_publish_time`; the publish date must be between 10 minutes and 30 days from the request — [Meta: Pages API Posts](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/pages-api/posts.md)
- Permissions: `pages_manage_engagement`, `pages_manage_posts`, `pages_read_engagement`, `pages_read_user_engagement`, plus `publish_video` for video — [Meta: Pages API Posts](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/pages-api/posts.md)
- Reels can only be published to Pages, not profiles or Groups — [Zernio guide](https://zernio.com/blog/schedule-facebook-posts-via-api)

Threads API:
- Rolling 24-hour limits: 250 API-published posts (carousels count as one), 1,000 replies, 100 deletions, 500 location searches; usage via `GET /{threads-user-id}/threads_publishing_limit` — [Meta: Threads API Overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/threads/overview)
- Text max 500 characters; images JPEG/PNG up to 8MB, width 320-1440px, aspect up to 10:1; video MOV/MP4 (H264/HEVC, AAC), 23-60 fps, max 300 seconds, 1GB, 100 Mbps; carousel 2-20 children — [Meta: Threads API Overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/threads/overview)
- Scopes: `threads_basic`, `threads_content_publish`, `threads_manage_replies`, `threads_delete`, `threads_location_tagging` — [Meta: Threads API Overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/threads/overview)

LinkedIn (Community Management API / Posts API):
- Posts API replaces ugcPosts; organic content types supported: text, images, videos, documents, article, MultiImage, poll, celebration; carousels are sponsored-only (organic carousel not supported); Marketing Version 202511 sunsets November 16, 2026; all calls require `Linkedin-Version: YYYYMM` and `X-Restli-Protocol-Version: 2.0.0` headers — [Microsoft Learn: Posts API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/shares/posts-api)
- Article posts: no URL scraping; partner must supply title, description and thumbnail (uploaded via Images API) — [Microsoft Learn: Posts API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/shares/posts-api)
- Permissions: `w_organization_social` (page roles ADMINISTRATOR / DIRECT_SPONSORED_CONTENT_POSTER / CONTENT_ADMIN), `r_organization_social`, `w_member_social`, `r_member_social` (restricted, approved users only); targeted organic posts need audience > 300 members; `lifecycleState` PUBLISHED is the only accepted value at creation (no native scheduling) — [Microsoft Learn: Posts API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/shares/posts-api)
- Community Management API has a Development tier (default) and Standard tier; Development tier limits: 500 calls per app and 100 calls per member per 24 hours, no BATCH_GET, no Social Actions webhooks; developers are expected to build core use cases within 12 months; default per-app limit was raised from 100 to 500 — [Microsoft Learn: CM API Migration Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/community-management-api-migration-guide); [Microsoft Learn: CM Overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/community-management-overview)
- Standard tier upgrade requires completed Development-tier integration, a screencast, and verification of the app by a LinkedIn Page of the same organization — [Microsoft Learn: Increasing Access](https://learn.microsoft.com/eu-es/linkedin/marketing/increasing-access)

X (Twitter) API:
- X API v2 is now pay-per-usage, credit-based, "no contracts, subscriptions, or minimum spend"; Post: Create $0.015 per request; Post: Create (with URL) $0.200; Post: Create (summoned) $0.010; pay-per-usage plans capped at 3 million post reads per monthly billing cycle (Enterprise above that); read prices $0.005 per post, $0.010 per user — [X: Pricing](https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/pricing); [X: About X API](https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/about-x-api)
- Deduplication: the same resource requested twice within 24 hours is charged once — [X: About X API](https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/about-x-api)

TikTok Content Posting API:
- Unaudited clients: all content is restricted to private viewing (privacy_level forced to SELF_ONLY), up to 5 users may post per 24 hours, and accounts must be private at posting time; lifting this requires a Content Posting API audit separate from app review; audited clients still have a 24-hour active-creator cap based on usage estimates in the audit form and a per-creator posting cap — [TikTok: Content Sharing Guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines); [Postiz TikTok docs](https://docs.postiz.com/self-host/providers/tiktok); [PostEverywhere guide](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-to-get-social-media-api-access)
- Guidelines require the client be intended for a wide audience, not internal/private use — [TikTok: Content Sharing Guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/content-sharing-guidelines)

YouTube Data API:
- Default quota 10,000 units/day for all endpoints except `search.list` and `videos.insert`, which each have their own bucket limited to 100 calls per day; `videos.insert` costs 1 unit; `videos.update`/`videos.delete`/`playlistItems.insert` 50 units; `captions.insert` 400; quotas reset at midnight Pacific Time; every request (even invalid) costs at least 1 unit — [Google: YouTube quota costs](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/determine_quota_cost)

Pinterest API v5:
- Trial access: pins and boards created are visible only to their creator (Sandbox entities); rate limited per day per app (universal cap 1,000 requests/day cited on rate-limits page); Standard access: universal limit 100 requests/second per user per app with per-minute category limits; upgrade requires an approved Trial app, developer-guidelines compliance, and a video demo showing the OAuth flow and that sensitive data is not stored — [Pinterest: Access Tiers](https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/key-concepts/access-tiers/); [Pinterest: Rate Limits](https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/reference/rate-limits/)
- Apps registered on or after September 14, 2026 may receive HTTP 403 `PINNER_DATA_ACCESS_DENIED` when requesting boards/pins of non-business users without authorization — [Pinterest: Access Tiers](https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/key-concepts/access-tiers/)

Bluesky / AT Protocol (secondary sources only; official page could not be fetched):
- Write limits are points-based: 5,000 points per account per hour; createRecord costs 3 points (update 2); roughly 1,666 posts/hour ceiling; createSession limited to 30 per 5 minutes and 300 per day; a 24-hour block follows repeated violations; generic 429 responses — [TimeToPost guide](https://timetopost.co/blog/post-to-bluesky-api); [AdaptlyPost](https://adaptlypost.com/pt/blog/bluesky-api-rate-limit); [atproto GitHub discussion](https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/discussions/697)

Google Business Profile API:
- Default 300 QPM for Business Information, Account Management, Performance, Verifications, Lodging, Place Actions and Notifications APIs; edits limited to 10 per minute per Google Business Profile (cannot be increased); Create Location 300 QPD; quota errors return HTTP 429; increases typically denied if average usage is below 50% of current limit; a quota of 0 means access not granted, apply via "Application For Basic API Access" — [Google: GBP Usage Limits](https://developers.google.com/my-business/support/limits)
- Local posts via `mybusiness.googleapis.com/v4/accounts/{accountId}/locations/{locationId}/localPosts`; types include EVENT and OFFER (with coupon code, redemption URL, terms); CTA types BOOK, ORDER, SHOP, LEARN_MORE, SIGN_UP, CALL; Product posts cannot be created via API — [Google: GBP Posts](https://developers.google.com/my-business/content/posts-data)
- Third-party status (verify): localPosts and reviews still run on v4 and are active; Q&A API discontinued 3 November 2025 — [SlashPost](https://slashpost.ai/blogs/google-business-profile/google-business-profile-api-documentation-2026)

### Inferences
- A scheduler must implement its own queue and rate-limit accounting for Instagram (100/24h), Threads (250/24h), YouTube (100 uploads/day shared across all tenants on one GCP project, which forces per-tenant project strategy or quota-increase applications), and LinkedIn Development tier (500 calls/app/day, effectively blocking production use until Standard-tier approval).
- X publishing is now a direct variable cost ($0.015-$0.20 per post) that must be passed through or capped in pricing.
- TikTok and LinkedIn each require a second-stage review (Content Posting audit; Standard tier) after basic app approval; plan 2-3 months of lead time and build with those channels gated as "beta".
- Native scheduling exists only on Facebook Pages (10 min - 30 days); all other networks require server-side scheduling.

- Bluesky official rate-limit page redirected to bsky.network and returned empty; figures are from secondary sources.
- Instagram page internally conflicts (100 vs 50 posts/24h); treat 50 as a conservative planning number for carousel-heavy accounts.
- Facebook Pages API numeric publishing quota is not documented on the fetched page; only the Graph API generic BUC rate-limit headers apply.
- LinkedIn Standard-tier numeric limits are not published (shown per-app in the Developer Portal). Whether LinkedIn newsletters/articles can be published via third-party API was not confirmed; the Posts API "article" type is a link post, not a native LinkedIn Article or newsletter.
- Did not retrieve Pinterest numeric category limits or the GBP localPost summary character limit from official pages.

## Key Question 4: Token lifetimes and refresh rules per platform

### Takeaway
Meta long-lived user tokens (Facebook and Instagram Login) last ~60 days and must be proactively refreshed; long-lived Page tokens do not expire on a schedule; LinkedIn gives 60-day access tokens plus 365-day refresh tokens only to approved Marketing Developer Platform partners, after which the member must re-authorize.

### Cited Findings
- Meta Facebook Login: short-lived User and Page tokens expire "in hours"; long-lived User tokens last about 60 days (`expires_in` 5,183,944 s); long-lived Page tokens have no fixed expiration and "only expire or are invalidated under certain conditions"; expired tokens cannot be exchanged (user must log in again); exchange via `GET oauth/access_token?grant_type=fb_exchange_token` server-side; SDK auto-refresh only if the person used the app within the last 90 days — [Meta: Long-lived tokens](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/facebook-login/guides/access-tokens/get-long-lived)
- Instagram API with Instagram Login: long-lived token 60 days; refresh via `GET https://graph.instagram.com/refresh_access_token?grant_type=ig_refresh_token`; token must be at least 24 hours old, not expired, and the user must have granted `instagram_business_basic`; tokens not refreshed within 60 days expire and cannot be refreshed; authorization code valid 1 hour, single use — [Meta: Business Login for Instagram](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/business-login)
- LinkedIn: access tokens valid 60 days by default; programmatic refresh tokens valid 365 days (fixed TTL from initial auth, not extended on use); available to approved Marketing Developer Platform partners; member must reauthorize when the refresh token expires; LinkedIn may revoke tokens at any time and apps must fall back to the login flow; tokens ~500 chars, plan for 1,000 — [Microsoft Learn: Refresh Tokens](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/shared/authentication/programmatic-refresh-tokens)
- Pinterest: continuous refresh model (access token 30 days / refresh token 365 days) is widely described by integrators but not stated on the fetched access-tiers page — [Pinterest: Access Tiers](https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/key-concepts/access-tiers/) (token lifetimes not mentioned; see Gaps)
- Bluesky: reuse refresh tokens rather than calling createSession per post (30 per 5 min / 300 per day limit) — [TimeToPost guide](https://timetopost.co/blog/post-to-bluesky-api)

### Inferences
- A background job must refresh Instagram-Login tokens between day 1 and day 59 and Meta Facebook-Login user tokens before day 60; storing and using long-lived Page tokens avoids the Instagram 60-day cliff for Facebook-connected IG accounts.
- LinkedIn connections without MDP partner approval will hard-expire every 60 days with no refresh path, so the UX must handle re-authorization prompts; applying for Marketing Developer Platform / Community Management Standard tier is a launch prerequisite.

### Gaps
- Threads token lifetime (reported elsewhere as 60-day long-lived with refresh) was not on the fetched overview page.
- Pinterest, YouTube (Google OAuth refresh tokens, 7-day expiry for apps in "Testing" publishing status), TikTok (24-hour access / 365-day refresh per integrator docs) and X OAuth 2.0 (2-hour access with refresh) lifetimes were not verified against official pages in this pass.

## Key Question 7: Compliance context (EU AI Act, India DPDP, Meta AI labels)

### Takeaway
EU AI Act Article 50 transparency duties applied from 2 August 2026 (machine-readable marking for pre-existing generative systems deferred to 2 December 2026); India's DPDP Rules were notified 13 November 2025 with phased obligations through 14 November 2026 and 14 May 2027; Meta labels AI content via "AI info" using C2PA detection and user disclosure, and exposes `is_ai_generated` in the Instagram publishing API.

### Cited Findings
EU AI Act:
- Article 50 transparency obligations took effect 2 August 2026, applying to in-scope systems regardless of when placed on the market; content published before that date need not be retroactively labelled — [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-03-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-take-effect-2-august-2026); [Institute of Directors Ireland PDF](https://www.iodireland.ie/images/uploads/downloads/EU_AI_Act_Transparency_Obligations_Take_Effect_on_2_August_2026.pdf)
- Grace period until 2 December 2026 for machine-readable marking/detection of AI-generated content by generative systems already on the market before 2 August 2026 — [Potter Clarkson](https://www.potterclarkson.com/news/eu-ai-act-article-50-new-ai-transparency-rules); [GDPR Local](https://gdprlocal.com/eu-ai-act-article-50/)
- Scope: AI-interaction disclosure, synthetic content marking (Art. 50(2)), deepfake and public-interest text disclosure by deployers, emotion recognition/biometric categorisation — [Future of Life Institute guide](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/transparency-rules-article-50/); [A&L Goodbody](https://www.algoodbody.com/insights-publications/guide-to-article-50-of-the-eu-ai-act)
- Commission published a voluntary Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content and optional EU labelling icons — [BSI press release](https://www.bsigroup.com/en-US/insights-and-media/media-center/press-releases/2026/august/eu-ai-act-article-50-transparency-obligations/); [McCann FitzGerald](https://www.mccannfitzgerald.com/knowledge/technology-and-innovation/one-month-to-go-eu-ai-act-transparency-compliance)
- Breaches fall under the second penalty tier (Art. 99(4)(g)) — [Kovrr](https://www.kovrr.com/glossary-ai/eu-ai-act-article-50-transparency-obligations)

India DPDP:
- MeitY notified the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 on 13 November 2025 (one source says 14 Nov), bringing the DPDP Act 2023 into force in phases — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1708164/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-notified); [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/indias-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023-brought-into-force-)
- Phased milestones: second tranche of provisions 14 November 2026; core consent, notice, breach and Significant Data Fiduciary obligations fully in force 14 May 2027 (18-month runway) — [Mondaq 2026 milestones](https://www.mondaq.com/dpdp-act-and-rules-2025-the-2026-compliance-milestones-businesses-cant-afford-to-miss/1830402); [Uniqus timelines](https://uniqus.com/digital-personal-data-protection-act-timelines/)
- Significant Data Fiduciaries face India-based DPO and independent audit duties before May 2027; breach notification windows cited as 72 hours to affected Data Principals in some summaries — [Seclore guide](https://www.seclore.com/fundamentals/dpdp-rules-2025-compliance-guide/); [Tsaaro](https://tsaaro.com/dpdpa-compliance)

Meta AI labelling:
- Meta announced AI-image labelling across Facebook, Instagram and Threads in February 2024, requiring users to disclose photorealistic AI video or realistic audio, with penalties for non-disclosure — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/02/06/meta-ai-labeling-fix-oversight-board); [AI Business](https://aibusiness.com/responsible-ai/meta-to-label-ai-generated-content-across-facebook-instagram)
- "Made with AI" label replaced by "AI info" (2024) because minor retouching was being labelled; detected edits show the label in a menu, generated content is labelled visibly — [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/meta-changes-ai-generated-content-label_id160026)
- Ads: Meta automatically applies "AI info" when advertisers use Background Generation, Image Generation or Add Animation, and uses C2PA metadata to detect third-party generative AI — [Marketing Dive](https://marketingdive.com/news/sociable-meta-adds-updated-disclosure-tags-for-ai-generated-ads/824833); [Social Media Today](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-adds-updated-disclosure-tags-for-ai-generated-ads/824658)
- Instagram publishing API exposes `is_ai_generated=true` for self-disclosure — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing)

### Inferences
- A SaaS that generates captions/images with AI for EU customers should (a) embed C2PA/machine-readable provenance in generated media by 2 Dec 2026 at the latest, (b) set `is_ai_generated` on Instagram where images are AI-generated, and (c) log AI involvement per post for deployer disclosure duties.
- Storing Indian end-customer data (inbox DMs, review requesters, WhatsApp contacts) makes the SaaS a Data Fiduciary (or processor) under DPDP; consent records, breach-notification tooling and data-deletion flows should be live before 14 May 2027, with the 14 Nov 2026 tranche as an interim checkpoint.

### Gaps
- Primary legal texts (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 Art. 50; India Gazette G.S.R. notification) were not fetched; dates come from consistent law-firm summaries.
- No 2025-2026 Meta policy document on organic AI-content labelling was found; current newsroom wording not verified. The "14% of ads rejected for undisclosed AI" statistic appears in a single vendor blog and is unverified.
