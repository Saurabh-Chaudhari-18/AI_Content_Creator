# Enterprise & Mid-Market Social Media Management Platforms: Feature Inventory (Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Sprinklr Social, Emplifi, Brandwatch SMM), 2025–2026

Research date: 2026-11-04. All prices are as shown on vendor pages or secondary sources on that date unless a different date is noted. Note on sourcing: G2 and Capterra pages returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches, so review-complaint data below comes from search-result summaries of those pages and from third-party aggregators (several of them competitor-authored); treat tallies as directional.

## Key Question 1: What is included at each pricing tier, and what is the entry price per user/month?

### Takeaway
Two vendors publish per-seat prices: Hootsuite (Standard from $99/user/mo annual, Professional from $199, Advanced up to $399, Enterprise custom) and Sprout Social (Essentials $79 annual / $99 monthly, Standard $199, Professional $299, Advanced $399 per seat, Enterprise custom). Emplifi publishes per-account (unlimited-user) prices: Social Marketing Essential "starting at" $1,249/mo and Advanced $2,499/mo. Sprinklr discontinued its self-serve plans (formerly ~$249–$359/user/mo) with customer access ending April 30, 2026, and now is quote-only; Brandwatch is quote-only with no published prices.

## Key Question 2: What approval workflow capabilities exist (multi-step, external/client approvals, mobile approval, comment threads on drafts)?

### Takeaway
Approval workflows are gated to upper tiers: Hootsuite's Advanced plan (with guest approvers added May 2026 and a Planner "filter by approver" added June 2026); Sprout's Advanced plan is required for external approvers (max 3, email-based, with automated reminders). Sprinklr offers automated approve/escalate/block workflows with AI compliance flagging; Emplifi lists Multi-Stage Approval Workflows; Brandwatch lets you "define who approves your content" within the calendar.

### Cited Findings
- Hootsuite: approval workflows, internal comments, content collaboration, and suspending scheduled posts start at the Advanced plan — [Hootsuite Plans](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans)
- Hootsuite added the ability to invite guest approvers in May 2026 — [Hootsuite May 2026 features](https://blog.hootsuite.com/new-features-may-2026/)
- Hootsuite June 2026: "Planner: Filter by approver" — filter the content calendar to see which posts are waiting on specific people for approval — [Hootsuite June 2026 features](https://blog.hootsuite.com/new-features-june-2026/)
- Hootsuite supports adding Instagram alt text during scheduling and approvals, including for carousels (third-party Dec 2025 recap) — [3l3c.ai Hootsuite Dec 2025 updates](https://www.3l3c.ai/blog/ai-marketing-tools-for-small-business/hootsuite-updates-small-business)
- Sprout: External approvers only on the Advanced plan, up to three; Link Sharing must be enabled first; approvers added by email and act through email prompts; Sprout sends automated reminder emails before deadlines; external approvers don't receive emails for every post — [Sprout: social media approval](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-approval/); [Sprout features](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/sprout-features/)
- Sprout internal workflow: approvers configured under Approval Workflows in account settings; choose "Any or All" sign-off per step; users with Full Publishing permission cannot skip a step unless the workflow allows it — [Sprout support: Configuring Your Account](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/9385327882125)
- Sprinklr: governance includes "granular access controls, built-in audit trails, and automated approval workflows"; workflows can "automatically approve, escalate and block posts"; AI flags "violations, brand risks and non-compliant content" before posts go live — [Sprinklr Social](https://www.sprinklr.com/products/social-media-management/)
- Emplifi: "Multi-Stage Approval Workflows" listed on pricing page (tier unclear); Advanced mentions "advanced team workflows and brand controls"; Intelligent mentions enterprise-wide governance — [Emplifi Pricing](https://emplifi.io/pricing)
- Emplifi AI Composer docs: "human moderation is required" and AI Composer output cannot be deployed automatically; product page describes a loop where teams give direction, Fuel AI drafts, and reviewers approve — [Emplifi AI Composer docs](https://docs.emplifi.io/platform/latest/home/ai-composer); [Emplifi AI Composer product page](https://emplifi.io/products/publisher/ai-composer)
- Brandwatch: content approval flows are part of the calendar; "Define who approves your content before it goes out" under User roles — [Brandwatch SMM](https://www.brandwatch.com/products/social-media-management/)

### Inferences
- Sprout and Hootsuite both lock approvals behind their ~$399/seat tier, making approval-enabled plans expensive for agencies with many client approvers; Sprout's 3-external-approver cap is a notable constraint for agencies.
- Sprinklr is the only vendor explicitly describing AI-driven compliance blocking inside the approval chain.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed whether any of the five supports approving posts in the mobile app specifically, or threaded comments on drafts beyond Hootsuite's "internal comments" line item.
- Emplifi's approval-stage count and tier gating could not be read from the pricing page.
- Brandwatch's approval mechanics (multi-step, external) are not documented on public pages fetched.

## Key Question 4: What brand management features exist (brand kits, brand voice profiles, asset libraries, compliance/banned-word checks)?

### Takeaway
Asset libraries are universal (Sprout asset library + Canva/Dropbox/Google Drive, Emplifi Content Collections with unlimited storage and Google Drive/Box/Dropbox, Brandwatch "Content pool", Sprinklr DAM integrations and shared content libraries, Hootsuite content library). Brand-voice training is explicit at Emplifi (Brand Voice, ≥5 posts) and claimed by Sprinklr ("brand risks and non-compliant content" flagging); Sprout exposes "Blocked Words" in its Influencer Marketing product; Hootsuite Enterprise offers "custom compliance solutions."

- Emplifi: unlimited storage, Content Collections, Asset Editor, cloud storage connections (Google Drive, Box, Dropbox), DAM Integration, AI Image Scoring (tier mapping unclear) — [Emplifi Pricing](https://emplifi.io/pricing)
- Emplifi Brand Voice: generates text in a profile's voice, Enterprise package or paid add-on for Business package — [Emplifi AI Composer docs](https://docs.emplifi.io/platform/latest/home/ai-composer)
- Sprinklr: AI flags "violations, brand risks and non-compliant content" before posts go live; "Shared content libraries"; integrations with DAM systems and Adobe Experience Manager — [Sprinklr Social](https://www.sprinklr.com/products/social-media-management/)
- Brandwatch: "Content pool" — "One digital asset library to store all your content" — [Brandwatch SMM](https://www.brandwatch.com/products/social-media-management/)
- Hootsuite Enterprise: "Single sign-on (SSO) and custom compliance solutions"; Advanced: logos/imagery on reports — [Hootsuite Plans](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans)

### Inferences
- Only Emplifi documents a trainable brand-voice model with explicit data requirements; others describe tone adjustment or compliance flagging but not voice training on the brand's own corpus.

### Gaps
- No vendor page fetched describes a formal "brand kit" (colors/fonts/logos) feature; Capterra lists "digital asset management" for Sprinklr's former self-serve plan but no detail.
- Hootsuite's content library and brand-guideline tooling details were not found on the plans page.
- Banned-word/compliance checks for Hootsuite, Emplifi, and Brandwatch not documented in fetched sources.

## Key Question 6: Inbox/engagement, analytics, listening, roles/permissions, and integrations by vendor

### Takeaway
Hootsuite and Sprout tier their inbox/analytics features heavily (sentiment, routing, CSAT, spike alerts at $399 tiers; social listening is an add-on at Sprout and Enterprise-grade at Hootsuite). Sprinklr and Brandwatch sell listening as sibling products (Sprinklr Insights; Brandwatch Consumer Intelligence with "100 million+ sources", 12 months consumer data, 7 years competitive channel analytics). Emplifi's listening is add-on based (Reddit, crisis/AI spike alerts, query copilot). Integrations: Salesforce is common (Hootsuite Enterprise, Sprout, Sprinklr, Emplifi Care Advanced, Brandwatch); Sprinklr adds Microsoft Dynamics, AEM, data lakes, "100+ integrations."

### Cited Findings
- Hootsuite inbox: unified inbox (Standard); automated replies, DM workflows, AI smart replies, auto-tagging, contact profiles (Professional); assignment, auto-routing, skill-based routing, CSAT surveys, productivity tracking (Advanced); advanced inbox + Salesforce (Enterprise). Listening: basic (Standard); Quick Search 30 days (Professional); 13 months history, Lumen-powered, alerts (Enterprise). Analytics: 5 → 20 competitors benchmarking; custom/scheduled reports from Professional; attribution/ROI at Enterprise — [Hootsuite Plans](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans)

## Key Question 8: What do recent reviews cite as the main complaints?

### Takeaway
Price/per-seat value is the dominant complaint across Hootsuite and Sprout; Hootsuite also draws criticism for slow support and a steep learning curve (G2 support score 7.9 vs Sprout 8.8 per Sprout's own comparison); Sprout for "clunky" UI and cost; Sprinklr for complexity ("Complex Usage" 54 mentions, "Learning Curve" 48 on G2 per a third-party tally), cost, and slow sales-led onboarding; Emplifi for limited analytics depth, inconsistent sentiment accuracy, and no transparent pricing; Brandwatch SMM for an outdated-looking UI, disconnecting accounts, and failed posts (small sample).

### Cited Findings
- Reddit (r/SocialMediaManagers, Jan 2026 thread analysis): 8 of 18 substantive comments came from vendors; pricing is the recurring theme; a nonprofit buyer concluded Hootsuite costs more than Sprout at comparable tiers; across Sprout Reddit mentions 40% raise price, 35% positive — [NapoleonCat Reddit analysis](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-reddit/)
- Hootsuite complaint quoted from Reddit/G2: "why do I have to pay for even standard features?" — [Statusbrew Hootsuite alternatives](https://statusbrew.com/insights/Hootsuite-alternatives)
